# nightstand — Production Log

## Config
- Type: prop (static furniture), game asset for Knitting Empire (Godot 4.7)
- Target: GLB in assets/props/, uncompressed (Godot cannot import Draco)
- Tier: quick (the second batch of house furniture, eight pieces at once; the interiors' lighter loop)
- Style: stylised, hand-painted, cozy: the first batch of house furniture (`build.py`) is the template
- Mode: auto, headless

## Brief
2026-11-14, from the session building out Builder's Delight's catalogue: Alex wants house design and expansion to be a big part of the game, with a comprehensive catalogue of things to buy for the house. The catalogue had no real bedroom, bath or kitchen-storage pieces.
- Nightstand (Bed & Study tab, footprint 0.5 x 0.5 m): tapered legs, a drawer with a brass knob, a lower shelf with two books, a sage table lamp with a glowing shade and a cup of tea on its saucer.

## Reference Image
- Path: none. Built to match the first batch of house furniture (warm orange painted wood, tartan, cream ceramics, brass) rather than a reference.

## Source
- Method: scripted, `art/world_assets/house_furniture/build.py` (`nightstand()`), helpers in `kelib.py`, `craftlib.py` and `knit.py`; textures generated into `nightstand/tex/` (painterly wood and cloth, knitted stitch maps, patchwork)

## Pipeline
- Export: GLB 0.21 MB, 1596 triangles, 11 materials, size 0.5 x 0.446 x 1.001 m (x, depth, height)
- UV check (kiln `tools/uv_check.py`): WARN only (overlap and flips from box projection on tiling textures, expected); 0 % collapsed after `uv_rest()`
- Light bake: `art/world_assets/light_bake/build.py -- nightstand` (Cycles, reach 0.6 m), then imported
