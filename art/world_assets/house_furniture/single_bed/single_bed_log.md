# single_bed — Production Log

## Config
- Type: prop (static furniture), game asset for Knitting Empire (Godot 4.7)
- Target: GLB in assets/props/, uncompressed (Godot cannot import Draco)
- Tier: quick (the second batch of house furniture, eight pieces at once; the interiors' lighter loop)
- Style: stylised, hand-painted, cozy: the first batch of house furniture (`build.py`) is the template
- Mode: auto, headless

## Brief
2026-11-14, from the session building out Builder's Delight's catalogue: Alex wants house design and expansion to be a big part of the game, with a comprehensive catalogue of things to buy for the house. The catalogue had no real bedroom, bath or kitchen-storage pieces.
- Single Bed (Bed & Study tab, footprint 1.0 x 2.0 m): turned posts with ball finials, a spindle headboard and a panelled footboard, a ticking mattress, a pillow, a turned-down sheet and a blue knitted blanket with cream stripes, dropped over it by cloth simulation (`drape()`).

## Source
- Method: scripted, `art/world_assets/house_furniture/build.py` (`single_bed()`), helpers in `kelib.py`, `craftlib.py` and `knit.py`; textures generated into `single_bed/tex/` (painterly wood and cloth, knitted stitch maps, patchwork)

## Pipeline
- Export: GLB 0.53 MB, 4189 triangles, 5 materials, size 1.008 x 2.005 x 1.149 m (x, depth, height)
- UV check (kiln `tools/uv_check.py`): WARN only (overlap and flips from box projection on tiling textures, expected); collapsed 1.8 to 2.8 %: the cloth's turned-under hem, which takes its edge's colour on purpose (drape())
- Light bake: `art/world_assets/light_bake/build.py -- single_bed` (Cycles, reach 0.8 m), then imported
