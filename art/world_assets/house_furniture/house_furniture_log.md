# house_furniture — build log

Eleven pieces of furniture for Nora's house, rebuilt in the style of Alex's prop references. They
replace box models that had not changed since the game was first built (`scripts/catalog.gd`).

## Brief (Alex, 2026-11-11)

"Recreate assets we haven't changed since the initial creation in the style of the attached. First prompt
the open ChatGPT tab for a reference image in the style of the attached for the object on a white
background, then use that reference image to inform how you build it in Blender. Make at least 10
assets in order of what you feel are most vital, then insert them into the game. Skip the final two
passes to save on token costs. Focus especially on assets in the main character's house area."

## Method

1. Reference: one prompt per piece in Alex's ChatGPT conversation that made the bench and barn
   references ("now a ... in this style ... on a plain white background"). The lamp and the fern share
   one image. Screenshots of the results are in `ref/` (the wardrobe's shows only its top half).
2. Build: `build.py`, one function per piece, on the box model's footprint and facing, sized to Nora
   (seat cushions 0.42 m up). Painterly craftlib textures for wood, cloth and stone, a tartan made here,
   flat colours for brass, iron, ceramic, flames.
3. Review: one render of each on a sheet against the references, then one fix pass (`review/pass2/sheet.png`).
   The two further passes the method normally asks for were skipped, as asked.
4. Game: models in `assets/props/`, `model` lines in `data/furniture.json`, the fireplace's flames and
   firelight and the lamp's light added in `Catalog.build`, seat heights in `main.gd` `_seat_point`.

## Fix pass (pass 1 → pass 2)

- The plaid was a big two-colour gingham → a red tartan with cream and gold lines, as in the references.
- The fern lay flat → fronds that rise and arch, brighter greens, more of them.
- The sofa's throw cut into the back cushion → laid over the back and down the cushion's face.

## The throw and the tartan (2026-11-12)

Alex: "the blanket on the sofa looks like cardboard, upload some fabric textures and physics from the
web." (He wrote "feb"; read as "web".)

- The throw was three flat slabs. It is now a sheet dropped onto the sofa and settled by Blender's cloth
  simulation (`drape()` in `build.py`: 90 frames, soft bending, the sofa's parts as obstacles), then cut
  to about 900 triangles, with a turned-under hem and a fringe of flat strands, red and cream by turns,
  along both short ends. The result is baked into the model; nothing is simulated in the game.
- The tartan is now 512 px and woven wool: its fibre speckle and its relief come from two Poly Haven
  fabric scans (CC0, in `../textures_web`, see the README there). The rocking chair's pads, the
  armchair's cushion and the table runner use the same tartan and were rebuilt with it.
- Renders in `review/pass3/`; in the game, `review/in_game/sofa.png`.

## Known differences left

- The fireplace's flames are the game's three glowing, flickering shapes, not the reference's painted flame.
- The wardrobe's lower half follows the top of its reference (the screenshot cut the rest).
- Still box models, not redone here: the two rugs, the flower pot and the sunflower patch.
