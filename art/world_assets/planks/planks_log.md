# planks — build log

Wall boards and floor planks for the houses, from two references of Alex's (`ref/wall_planks.png`: pale
vertical boards under a rail, over a skirting board; `ref/floor_planks.png`: warm oak planks with big knots).

## Brief (2026-11-12)

"Make these white wood planks to put on the wall and the wood planks to use as the flooring, make 5
variations of the wood grain so it looks more random and realistic, make the wood slightly darker than
the reference."

## What was made

- `grain.py` draws one board at a time by cutting a model of a tree: growth rings round a pith that
  wanders along the board, sliced at a changing depth (shallow gives the floor's cathedral figure, deep
  gives the wall's nearly straight lines), with knots that push the rings into an eye and sit in it with
  cracked hearts, fibre streaks, a bevelled edge and the gap to the next board.
- Five boards of each kind, side by side, are an atlas: `wall_planks.png` and `floor_planks.png` with
  their normal maps (640 x 1024 px, a board is 0.3 m wide and 2.6 m or 2.4 m long).
- Colours are the references' medians, about a tenth darker: wall `#e3cba9` to `#c6a884`, floor
  `#edae5e` to `#b0611d`.
- `build.py` (Blender) writes the atlases, copies them to `assets/textures/planks/`, and lays a mock-up
  (a boarded wall on a planked floor) the way the game lays them; `review.py` renders it.

## In the game

`scripts/planks.gd` (`Planks.lay`) builds one mesh per wall or floor, board by board: each takes one of
the five grains at random, half are mirrored, and floor boards are also turned end for end and have
their ends staggered from row to row. Wall boards are cut round the windows.

- Floors (`main.gd` `_plank_floor`): Nora's house and Rosewood Cottage, in place of the 20 flat-coloured
  plank boxes.
- Every other building has the same floor laid over its model's boards (Alex: "use the same flooring for
  the other buildings ... change the shade of wood to different colours for different buildings"):
  the shops in `interiors.gd`, the barn in `world.gd`. The shade is a stain on the same five boards
  (`Planks.SHADES`): house golden oak, cottage pale honey, Woolly Goods walnut, the bakery cherry, the
  land office grey driftwood, the barn weathered brown.
- Walls (`_wall_boards`): the inside face of every house wall (both faces of a room divider), in front
  of the timber posts and just behind the window frames, with a pale rail along the top and a skirting
  board. The outside stays plaster and timber.
- Walls not boarded: the low stubs shown when a wall is cut away, the strip above each door, and the
  shops and barn (their walls are part of their models).

## Review rounds

- Round 0 (atlas sheets): grain too fine and brown on the floor, too busy on the wall → wider rings,
  stronger figure, bigger knots and a more orange oak for the floor; deeper cut, lower contrast for the wall.
- Round 1 (`review/round2/` after the colour change): mock-up renders against the references.
- In the game (`review/in_game/`): the sunlit floor came out lighter and yellower than the reference
  (`#efbd62` against `#e9a55c`), the walls a little yellow → the floor's material is tinted down in
  `planks.gd` (`TINT`), and the wall boards' colours were made a touch cooler.
