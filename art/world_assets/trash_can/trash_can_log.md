# trash_can — build log

Park trash can copied from Alex's reference render (`ref/reference.png`), built by script (`build.py`)
the same way as the park bench (`../bench_park/bench_park_log.md`): proportions and details read from the
reference, shaders baked to textures, renders compared with the reference, then checked in the game.

## Brief

"Make trash cans and put next to benches where appropriate." The can only, no ground, leaves or plants.

## Inventory

- a tapered barrel of fourteen flat staves, wider at the top, their rounded tops standing above the upper band
- two iron bands that follow the taper, a dome rivet on every other stave
- a dark iron liner whose rim sits a little under the staves' tops
- two flat cast-iron posts, broad face to the front, narrow at the waist and wide at the band and the foot,
  on plinth feet; the barrel hangs between them, clear of the ground
- same wood, iron and steel as the bench (it uses the bench's shaders, with the grain running up each stave)

## Result

0.94 m wide over the feet, 0.60 m deep, 0.74 m tall: built at the size of the game's people, like the
resized bench. 4,432 triangles, two materials, 512 px colour and normal maps.

## Review rounds

- Round 1: the posts splayed out like tripod legs, and the staves had wide dark gaps → posts brought in and
  made stouter, staves widened and thickened, barrel made squatter.
- Round 2: the posts' broad face was turned sideways → turned to the front as in the reference, with the
  waist and the flare to the band and the foot; a rivet through the top, front and back.
- Round 3 (`review/round3/sheet.png`): nothing blocking found in my own reading.

## In the game

- `assets/props/trash_can.glb`, item `trash_can` in `data/furniture.json` (1.0 x 0.5 m of ground; not sold
  in the Build menu).
- Three stand by benches, at the benches' places in Alex's layout of 2026-11-11: the east end of the
  plaza's south bench, the north end of the bench he added east of the fountain, and past the lamp of the
  bench on the road to town. None at the plaza's north bench (a lamp at each end already), the short
  benches, the pond or the cottage garden. They are ordinary decor, so the world editor can move or delete them.
- The autotest checks the model and photographs up to three of them (`--only=bench` runs just that).
